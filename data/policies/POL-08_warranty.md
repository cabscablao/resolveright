# POL-08: Warranty

**Document ID:** POL-08 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Brightcart Customer Care

## Purpose

This policy sets out Brightcart's warranty for products that fail after the normal return period.

## Warranty periods

- **Electronics:** **12 months from the delivery date** for manufacturing defects.
- **Apparel and home goods:** **90 days from the delivery date** for workmanship defects, such as failed stitching or a broken seam.

The period is counted from the delivery date shown on the order.

## What is covered

The warranty covers failures caused by how the product was made. Examples are a speaker that stops working after months of normal use, or a seam that comes apart.

## What is not covered

The warranty **excludes accidental damage**, such as drops, spills, water damage, misuse, unauthorised repairs and normal wear and tear. Cosmetic changes from ordinary use are also excluded.

## Remedy

For a valid claim within the warranty period, the remedy is a **replacement**. If a replacement is not available, a refund or store credit may be offered. Warranty remedies do not carry the return-shipping fee, because the item has failed for a reason that is our responsibility.

## Expired warranty

If the claim is outside the warranty period, for example a defect reported 13 months after delivery of an electronic item, the agent cannot approve a replacement or refund. The agent politely explains that the warranty has ended. Repair services for out-of-warranty items are not covered by these policy documents, so the agent must not promise any.

## Evidence

The agent asks for a short description of the fault and when it started. Photos or video are helpful. Where the cause may be accidental damage, the agent escalates for review rather than denying outright.

## Approval limits

Agents may approve warranty replacements up to ₱5,000. Higher-value items go to manager approval (POL-02).

## Relationship to returns

Items within 30 days of delivery may also be eligible under POL-04. The agent chooses the policy that gives the customer the better lawful outcome.
