# POL-05: Shipping and Delivery

**Document ID:** POL-05 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Brightcart Customer Care

## Purpose

This policy describes Brightcart shipping options, fees and what to do when a delivery is late.

## Shipping methods and times

- **Standard shipping** takes **5 to 7 business days** from the ship date.
- **Express shipping** takes **2 business days** from the ship date.

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding Philippine public holidays. Processing time before an order ships is not counted. The estimated delivery date shown to the customer is calculated when the order ships.

## Shipping fees

Standard shipping is **free for orders over ₱1,650**. For orders of ₱1,650 or less, a flat standard shipping fee of **₱200** applies. Express shipping always has a fee.

## Late deliveries

A delivery is considered **late** when the estimated delivery date has passed without delivery. Support follows these steps:

1. If the delivery is **no more than 2 days late** (counted in calendar days from the estimated delivery date), tell the customer the package is still within a normal tolerance, share the tracking status, and ask them to wait.
2. If the delivery is **more than 2 days late**, apologise and **consider refunding the shipping fee** the customer paid. The agent may approve the shipping-fee refund. The order itself is not refunded and the package keeps moving.
3. If **7 or more days** have passed after the estimated delivery date with no delivery, the package is treated as lost and POL-06 applies.

If the customer paid no shipping fee, there is nothing to refund, and the agent only apologises and shares the tracking status.

## Delivery issues that are not lateness

Packages marked delivered but not received, or arriving at the wrong address, should be reported by the customer. The agent records the report and escalates when the case cannot be resolved with the order data available.

## Tracking information

Support agents can tell the customer the order status and the estimated delivery date, but they never promise a specific delivery day. Carrier delays caused by weather or public holidays do not change this policy.
