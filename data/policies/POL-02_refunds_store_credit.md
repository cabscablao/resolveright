# POL-02: Refunds and Store Credit

**Document ID:** POL-02 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Brightcart Customer Care

## Purpose

This policy describes the forms a resolution can take at Brightcart and who is allowed to approve them.

## Full and partial refunds

Brightcart issues a **full refund** when the problem is our error, for example a damaged item, a defective item, a wrong item, or a package lost in transit. A **partial refund** applies when only part of an order is returned, when an item shows wear, or when a deduction such as the return-shipping fee (POL-01) applies. Refunds are paid to the original payment method.

## Store credit alternative

A customer may choose **store credit** instead of a refund to the original payment method. Store credit carries a **10% bonus**: a ₱1,500.00 refund becomes ₱1,650.00 in credit. Store credit does not expire, is applied to the customer's Brightcart account, and cannot be converted back to cash once issued. Agents should offer store credit as an option but never push it.

## Shipping fees

Original shipping fees are refunded **only when the problem is our error**: damaged, defective or wrong items, lost packages, or a late delivery as described in POL-05. For ordinary change-of-mind returns the original shipping fee is not refunded.

## Approval authority

Support agents may approve refunds, store credit and replacements up to **₱5,000** per case. Anything **above ₱5,000 needs manager approval**. When a request exceeds the limit, the agent does not approve it. The recommended action is to escalate with a summary, and the customer is told that a manager will review the case.

## Combined amounts

The ₱5,000 limit applies to the total value of the resolution for the case, not to each item separately. A ₱3,000 refund plus a ₱2,500 shipping credit is ₱5,500 in total and needs manager approval.

## Refund timing

After approval, refunds are issued to the original payment method. Customers should expect them 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received, or 5 to 7 business days after approval when no return is required.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are not refundable, as described in POL-09.

## Documentation

Every approved resolution must record the order number, the policy cited, and the amount. Resolution amounts must never exceed what the order data shows was paid and not yet refunded.
