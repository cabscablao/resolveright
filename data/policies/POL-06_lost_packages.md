# POL-06: Lost Packages

**Document ID:** POL-06 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Brightcart Customer Care

## Purpose

This policy defines when a package is treated as lost and what Brightcart offers the customer.

## Definition of lost

A package is treated as **lost when 7 or more days have passed after the estimated delivery date** and the order still shows no delivery. The count starts on the estimated delivery date shown on the order. For example, if the estimated delivery date was 9 days ago and the package has not arrived, it is lost. If it was 1 day ago, the package is only late and POL-05 applies.

## Remedy

When a package is lost, the customer is offered a **replacement or a refund**, at the customer's choice. The refund includes the original shipping fee because the problem is our error (POL-02). No return of the item is needed. If the replacement item is out of stock, the refund or store credit is offered instead.

## Checks before approval

Before offering a remedy the agent confirms:

1. The order belongs to the customer who is asking.
2. The status is not delivered.
3. The estimated delivery date is at least 7 days in the past.

If the order shows as delivered but the customer says it never arrived, the agent does not approve a remedy and instead escalates for investigation, because carrier proof of delivery has to be checked.

## If the package turns up later

If the original package arrives after a replacement has been sent, the customer is asked to refuse or return it using a free label. Agents do not chase this case further.

## Approval limits

Agents may approve a replacement or refund up to ₱5,000. Above that, manager approval is needed (POL-02).

## What to tell the customer

The reply should apologise, confirm that the package is treated as lost under our policy, and ask whether the customer prefers a replacement or a refund. It should not guess at what the carrier did.
