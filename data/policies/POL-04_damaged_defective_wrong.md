# POL-04: Damaged, Defective and Wrong Items

**Document ID:** POL-04 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Brightcart Customer Care

## Purpose

This policy covers items that arrive broken, do not work, or are not what the customer ordered.

## Reporting deadlines

- **Damaged items** must be reported within **7 days of the delivery date**.
- **Defective items** (the item does not work as intended) must be reported within **30 days of the delivery date**. Longer-term defects may be covered by the warranty in POL-08.
- **Wrong items** (a different product, size or colour from the order) should be reported within 30 days of delivery.

## Evidence

For damage, the customer must provide **photos** of the item and of the packaging. The agent asks for photos before approving anything. For defects, a short description of the fault is enough. For wrong items, a photo of the item received is helpful but not required.

## Remedies

The customer may choose a **replacement or a refund**. If Brightcart is at fault, the refund includes the original shipping fee. Brightcart pays for the return, and the customer receives a **free return label** for wrong items and for damaged or defective items that need to be sent back. The return-shipping fee from POL-01 never applies to these cases.

## Final-sale items

Clearance or personalised items that are defective, damaged or wrong are still covered (see POL-03).

## Late claims

A damage claim reported **more than 7 days after delivery** cannot be approved by a support agent. The agent must escalate it to a human reviewer with a short case summary and must not promise any outcome to the customer. The same applies to defect claims after 30 days unless POL-08 warranty terms apply.

## Approval limits

Agents may approve resolutions up to ₱5,000. Higher-value claims, such as a ₱30,000 laptop, need manager approval and are escalated (POL-02, POL-10).

## Safety

If a defective product creates a safety risk, such as overheating or smoke, the agent stops normal processing and escalates immediately as urgent, following POL-10. The agent advises the customer to stop using the product.

## What to tell the customer

The reply should apologise, state what is needed next (photos, a choice of replacement or refund), and avoid promising approval before the checks are complete.
