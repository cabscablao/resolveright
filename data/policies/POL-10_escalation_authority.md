# POL-10: Escalation and Authority Limits

**Document ID:** POL-10 | **Version:** 1.1 | **Effective date:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Brightcart Customer Care

## Purpose

This policy lists the cases a support agent must not decide alone, and what an escalation contains.

## Always escalate

The agent must **always escalate** to a human in these situations:

1. **Suspected fraud**, such as a customer claiming a different identity or a pattern of repeated claims that looks abusive.
2. **Legal threats**, including mentions of lawyers, lawsuits, regulators or consumer-protection complaints.
3. **Safety hazards**, such as an overheating battery, smoke, fire, sparks or injury. These are **urgent**.
4. **Harassment** or abusive behaviour toward staff.
5. **Open chargebacks.** If the customer has disputed the charge with their bank, no refund is processed until it is resolved.
6. **Three or more refunds in the last 90 days** for the same customer.
7. **The customer asks for a human.** This request is always honoured.
8. **Claims above ₱5,000**, which need manager approval (POL-02).
9. **Late damage claims** reported more than 7 days after delivery (POL-04).
10. **Address changes with fraud indicators.** A request to change the delivery address of a processing order (POL-07) is escalated, not approved, when the order total is above ₱5,000, the order has an open chargeback, or the customer has three or more refunds in the last 90 days. A redirected parcel is hard to undo.

## Agent authority

Agents may approve resolutions up to **₱5,000**. They may not approve anything above this limit, override another policy, or make exceptions. If a case does not fit any policy, the agent does not guess: the case is escalated and the customer is told that the policy documents do not cover it.

## What an escalation contains

An escalation includes a short case summary, the order number, the reason code, the urgency level (normal or urgent), the policies that apply, and the facts found. Personal data is kept to the minimum needed.

## Telling the customer

The agent tells the customer that the case has been passed to a specialist, what happens next, and a realistic timeframe if one is available. The agent does not promise an outcome.

## Urgent cases

Safety hazards are marked urgent. The agent advises the customer to stop using the product and keep it away from flammable materials, and does not offer any resolution before the human review.

## No legal or medical advice

Agents do not give legal or medical advice. If asked, they decline politely and escalate where appropriate.
