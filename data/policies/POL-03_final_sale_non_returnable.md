# POL-03: Final-Sale and Non-Returnable Items

**Document ID:** POL-03 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Brightcart Customer Care

## Purpose

Some Brightcart products cannot be returned for a change of mind. This policy lists them and explains the exceptions.

## Non-returnable categories

The following items are **non-returnable** for change-of-mind requests:

- **Clearance items.** Products sold in the Clearance section are final sale. The product page and the order confirmation state this.
- **Personalised items.** Products made to order with a name, text or custom design, such as engraved or printed goods.
- **Gift cards.** Physical or digital gift cards (see also POL-09).
- **Opened consumables.** Food, supplements and similar consumable products once the seal has been opened.
- **Hygiene items.** Items such as personal-care devices, underwear and swimwear once the hygiene seal has been removed.

Unopened consumables that are still sealed follow the normal return rules in POL-01.

## Exceptions

The restriction does not apply when the item is **defective, damaged, or the wrong item was sent**. In those cases the claim is handled under POL-04 even if the product was clearance or personalised. A clearance lamp that arrives broken, for example, is eligible for replacement or refund. Warranty claims under POL-08 also remain available.

## Personalised items and errors

If a personalised item has an error, the agent must first establish **who made the error**. When Brightcart made the mistake, for example by printing a different name from the one on the order, it is treated as a wrong item. When the customer supplied the wrong spelling, the item is final sale. If it is unclear who made the error, the agent asks the customer for the original text they entered, and escalates if it still cannot be determined.

## What agents say to customers

When a request is declined because an item is final sale, the agent should explain the rule in plain words, mention the exceptions, and ask whether the item arrived damaged or defective. The agent must not make exceptions to this policy on their own authority.
