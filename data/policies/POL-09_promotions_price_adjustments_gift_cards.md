# POL-09: Promotions, Price Adjustments and Gift Cards

**Document ID:** POL-09 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Brightcart Customer Care

## Purpose

This policy covers promotional codes, requests for a price adjustment after a price drop, and gift cards.

## Price adjustments

If Brightcart itself lowers the price of an item, a customer who bought it within the last **14 days of purchase** may request a price adjustment. The agent refunds the difference between the price paid and the new price. For example, a coat bought for ₱6,650 whose price dropped to ₱5,350 earns a ₱1,300 adjustment. The refund goes to the original payment method.

Price adjustments apply only to **our own price drops**. They do not apply to prices at other retailers, to clearance markdowns on items that are already final sale (POL-03), or to purchases made more than 14 days earlier. The adjustment amount is never more than the price paid.

## Promotional codes

Promo codes **cannot be stacked**: only one code may be used per order. If a customer used a code and then wants to add another after the fact, the request is declined. A promo code cannot be applied to an order after it has been placed.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are **non-refundable** and **not redeemable for cash**. This applies to physical and digital cards, to cards bought by the customer, and to cards received as gifts. If a customer asks to return a gift card or to receive a refund for one, the agent declines politely. A gift card that is lost or stolen is escalated to a human, because the card number must be checked.

## Approval limits

Agents may approve price adjustments up to ₱5,000. For larger amounts, manager approval is needed (POL-02).

## Not covered here

These documents do not describe competitor price matching or loyalty points. If a customer asks about either, the agent says it is not covered in the policy documents and escalates, rather than guessing.

## What to tell the customer

Confirm the price paid, the new price, the amount to be adjusted and the timeline of 5 to 7 business days.
