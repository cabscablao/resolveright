# POL-01: Returns Policy

**Document ID:** POL-01 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Brightcart Customer Care

## Purpose

This policy explains when Brightcart accepts returns for change-of-mind reasons and how those returns are refunded. Returns for damaged, defective or wrong items follow POL-04 instead, and non-returnable items are listed in POL-03.

## Return window

Customers may return an eligible item within **30 days of the delivery date**. The window is counted from the day the carrier marks the order as delivered, not from the order date or the ship date. Day 30 is still inside the window; day 31 is outside it. Brightcart support agents may not extend the window. A request outside the window is declined politely, and the agent may mention that damaged or defective items are handled under different rules.

## Condition of returned items

Items must be unused and returned in their original packaging, with all accessories and tags. Items that show signs of wear, use or missing parts may be refused or refunded only in part, after review by the agent.

## Who pays for return shipping

For change-of-mind returns, the customer pays return shipping. Instead of asking the customer to prepay a label, Brightcart deducts a flat return-shipping fee from the refund: **₱200** for orders delivered within Metro Manila and **₱500** for orders delivered outside Metro Manila. Brightcart pays for return shipping only when the return is caused by our error, such as a wrong item or a damaged item (see POL-04).

## Orders delivered outside Metro Manila

For orders delivered outside Metro Manila, for example to Bulacan, Cavite or Batangas, the flat return-shipping fee is **₱500** instead of ₱200, because courier pickups in the provinces cost more. The agent states the fee that matches the delivery address of the order.

## Refund timing and method

Refunds go back to the **original payment method**. The refund is issued **5 to 7 business days after Brightcart receives the returned item** and confirms its condition. Customers who ask where their refund is should be told this timeline. Store credit is available as an alternative (see POL-02).

## Multi-item orders

When an order contains several items, only the returned item is refunded. The refund equals the price of that item minus the return-shipping fee (₱200 within Metro Manila, ₱500 outside). The other items stay with the customer and are not affected.

## Orders with earlier refunds

If part of the order has already been refunded, the new refund cannot exceed the amount still unrefunded on that order, minus the return-shipping fee.

## Approval limits

Agents may approve refunds up to ₱5,000. Refunds above that amount need manager approval (see POL-02 and POL-10).
